# Trackside website mockups — v2

Two refinements of the selected Trackside direction use a wide full bleed Ferrari hero without the previous gradient fade. The image fills the page width without distorting the car. Large white Futura headings sit on a solid racing-red card over the open left side of the photograph.

Both complete 1440 × 1440 desktop page mockups include navigation and My Garage, the Ferrari hero, one Discover/Save/Compare section, and a red footer with About, Terms, Contact, Privacy, copyright and a linked [Piratechs](https://piratechs.com/) credit. Open the [comparison grid](03-comparison.png) to see both directions together.

The original official straight thin wide logo remains unchanged. A small white carrier keeps its original navy/red wordmark visible in the header and footer. UI typography remains Futura Medium and Bold, as in the selected v1 Trackside. Brand colors remain navy `#07162F`, red `#E32636`, and white `#FFFFFF`.

The transparent version depicts the page at its top. If implemented, the intended header treatment becomes solid red after scrolling; these files are static mockups.

The shared Ferrari artwork is an unchanged copy of the generated v1 hero: a red Ferrari 458 Italia coupe, cream tan interior, polished silver five-spoke wheels, blue sky, green grass, and dark asphalt circuit. There is no photo tint or gradient; the solid red card stays beside the car. Generation details are preserved in [v1/prompts.md](../v1/prompts.md). No new image generation was needed for this refinement.

Page SVGs reference `media/hero-ferrari-458.png` and `media/logo.png`; keep `media/` beside them. PNGs include the images and chosen font faces. Earlier concepts and official brand assets are preserved.
