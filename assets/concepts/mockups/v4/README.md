## Directions

White Emotion sets “Chase the” in white and “Feeling” in red. A white circular action with a red arrow continues the lighter palette, with red labels and restrained editorial rules.

Scarlet Ribbon uses white headline lettering with a sharp red typographic band behind “Feeling.” The band sits in open sky above the car. A white vehicle rail and red details carry that treatment into the rest of the hero.

Each direction has a paired transparent-header and red-header version to compare the header treatment while retaining the same hero composition. Open the [hero comparison grid](05-hero-comparison.png) or the [full-page grid](06-full-page-comparison.png).

## Brand and artwork

The palette stays white `#FFFFFF`, racing red `#E32636` and navy `#07162F`, with navy used for small text where it helps readability. UI typography remains Futura Medium and Bold. White display lettering uses a fine navy edge where needed to remain legible over clouds.

Logos have transparent backgrounds and sit directly on the sky or red surfaces. The transparent header uses the original official logo. The contextual on-red logo from v3 changes only the Dreamer wordmark to white; the official crest, Driver lettering and typography stay intact. No white logo panels are used. Official brand files remain preserved.

The photograph is an unchanged copy of the selected red Ferrari 458 Italia scene with a cream tan cabin, polished silver five-spoke wheels, blue sky, green grass and dark asphalt circuit. No gradient or tint is placed over the car. Original generation details are in [v1/prompts.md](../v1/prompts.md); no new photo generation was needed for this round.

Each static page includes the header and menu, hero, one white editorial section, and a red footer with About, Terms, Contact, Privacy, 2026 copyright and a linked [Piratechs](https://piratechs.com/) credit. Page sources are editable SVGs; their `media/` folder must stay beside them. PNG previews include the artwork and selected font faces. Earlier concepts remain intact.
