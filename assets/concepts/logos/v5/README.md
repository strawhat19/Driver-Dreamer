# Driver Dreamer — Straight Bubbles and Wide Soft Weave, v5

This round retains the original **v1 Grand Tourer Crest** car, shield, crown and Driver Dreamer wordmark. Two variants explore the red thought trail beneath the car, continuing the solid bubbles from v4.

## Variants

| Variant | Thought trail | Editable artwork |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **01 — Straight Bubbles** | Three filled circles on one vertical centerline. | [Full logo](01-grand-tourer-straight-bubbles.svg) · [Icon](01-grand-tourer-straight-bubbles-icon.svg) |
| **02 — Wide Soft Weave** | Three wider rounded rectangles follow v4's subtle side-to-side weave. | [Full logo](02-grand-tourer-wide-soft-weave.svg) · [Icon](02-grand-tourer-wide-soft-weave-icon.svg) |

Straight Bubbles uses radii `10.5`, `6.5` and `3.5` at centers `(128, 176)`, `(128, 195)` and `(128, 210)`. Wide Soft Weave uses rectangles measuring `32 × 18`, `22 × 12` and `12 × 7`, with corner radii `6`, `4` and `2.5`, centered at `(128, 176)`, `(123, 195)` and `(130, 210)`. Both retain three separate shapes beneath the original car.

View the [comparison sheet](03-comparison.png) or [editable comparison SVG](03-comparison.svg) for both icons and wordmarks, enlarged thought-trail details, colors and fonts.

## Palette and typography

| Color | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep navy | `#07162F` | Shield, car details and Driver text |
| Racing red | `#E32636` | Crown, thought trail and Dreamer text |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Car silhouette |

The original live-text wordmark keeps the fallback stack `Avenir Next, Inter, Arial, sans-serif`, font size `66`, Driver weight `700`, Dreamer weight `750` and letter spacing `-2`. The displayed font depends on availability; the original SVG font stack remains the source.

## Files

Full-logo SVGs retain the `0 0 920 256` viewBox; icons retain `0 0 256 256`. Each has a matching transparent PNG: **1840 × 512** for full logos and **768 × 768** for icons. Earlier concept rounds and application assets remain untouched.
