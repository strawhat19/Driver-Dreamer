# Driver Dreamer — Thin Wide Weave and Straight Alignment, v7

This round pairs the selected v6 **Thin Wide Weave** with a straight version. Both retain the original Grand Tourer Crest car, shield, crown and Driver Dreamer wordmark.

## Alignment choices

| Variant | Arrangement | Editable artwork |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **01 — Thin Wide Weave** | An exact copy of v6 `02-grand-tourer-thin-wide-weave`, including its SVG and PNG files. The three rectangle centers remain `(128, 176)`, `(123, 195)` and `(130, 210)`. | [Full logo](01-grand-tourer-thin-wide-weave.svg) · [Icon](01-grand-tourer-thin-wide-weave-icon.svg) |
| **02 — Straight Thin Wide** | The same artwork with all three rectangles centered at `x=128`. Only the medium and small rectangles move horizontally. | [Full logo](02-grand-tourer-straight-thin-wide.svg) · [Icon](02-grand-tourer-straight-thin-wide-icon.svg) |

Both use rectangle sizes `32 × 10`, `22 × 7` and `12 × 4`, corner radii `5`, `3.5` and `2`, and vertical centers `176`, `195` and `210`. Straight Thin Wide changes the medium rectangle's `x` from `112` to `117` and the small rectangle's `x` from `124` to `122`; their other attributes remain unchanged.

View the [comparison sheet](03-comparison.png) or [editable comparison SVG](03-comparison.svg) for both icons, full wordmarks, enlarged thought trails, colors and fonts.

## Palette and typography

| Color | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep navy | `#07162F` | Shield, car details and Driver text |
| Racing red | `#E32636` | Crown, thought trail and Dreamer text |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Car silhouette |

The original live-text wordmark uses `Avenir Next, Inter, Arial, sans-serif` in that fallback order, font size `66`, Driver weight `700`, Dreamer weight `750` and letter spacing `-2`. The displayed typeface depends on available fonts; the original SVG font stack remains the source.

## Files

Full-logo SVGs retain the `0 0 920 256` viewBox; icons retain `0 0 256 256`. Each has a matching transparent PNG: **1840 × 512** for full logos and **768 × 768** for icons. Earlier concept rounds and application assets remain untouched.
