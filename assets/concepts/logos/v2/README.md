# Driver Dreamer — logo concepts, v2

Second round for **Driver Dreamer**, the app and database of luxury, sports and exotic vehicles. These are three new silhouettes following the DD track, car crest and layered catalog cards from v1.

## New directions

| Concept | Idea | Potential use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **01 — Horizon D** | A single D opened by a rising road: an invitation to the next drive. | App icon, collection cover, digital identity |
| **02 — Panorama GT** | A side-on grand tourer under a red horizon, with an open, welcoming silhouette. | Vehicle collection identity, masthead, enthusiast community |
| **03 — Precision Index** | A steering wheel opens into three index tabs, connecting driving with discovery. | Database app icon, search identity, saved collections |

## Palette

Racing red `#E32636`, deep navy `#07162F`, white `#FFFFFF`. Pale gray appears only in presentation surfaces.

## Files

Each numbered direction includes a transparent horizontal SVG logo with the exact **Driver Dreamer** wordmark, a square standalone `-icon.svg`, and matching transparent PNG exports. Full PNGs are 2200 × 480; icon PNGs are 960 × 960.

`04-comparison.svg` and `04-comparison.png` present the three concepts together. The comparison backgrounds and rounded presentation tiles are not baked into individual exports.

## Artwork notes

- Symbols are native editable SVG geometry. Wordmarks are vector outlines made from locally available fonts, so viewing does not require font installation.
- Each SVG has a useful viewBox and accessible title/description. Surrounding canvases are transparent; any solid surfaces within the mark are intentional.
- All vehicle geometry is original and generic; there are no manufacturer emblems or model-specific likenesses.
- These are concept assets for review. No app code or adopted brand assets were changed.
- Preserve v1 and v2. Start the next exploration in `assets/concepts/logos/v3/` following the Library `Logo_Mockup_Guides.md`.
