# Driver Dreamer — Original Crest, Solid Dream Bubbles, v4

This round keeps the selected **v1 Grand Tourer Crest** car, shield, crown and wordmark exactly as drawn. Three solid round red thought bubbles replace the collection rows beneath the car, using the bubble sizes from v3 **Sculpted GT**. The bubbles remain separate circles, with no surrounding cloud.

| Variant | Bubble arrangement | Editable artwork |

| **01 — Left Drift** | Mostly vertical, with lower bubbles slightly left of center. | [Full logo](01-grand-tourer-left-drift.svg) · [Icon](01-grand-tourer-left-drift-icon.svg) |
| **02 — Right Drift** | Mostly vertical, with lower bubbles slightly right of center. | [Full logo](02-grand-tourer-right-drift.svg) · [Icon](02-grand-tourer-right-drift-icon.svg) |

All three use radii `10.5`, `6.5` and `3.5`, with vertical centers `176`, `195` and `210`. The largest bubble stays at horizontal center `128`; the medium/small centers are `124/120` for Left Drift, `132/136` for Right Drift and `123/130` for Soft Weave. Each can serve as an app icon, avatar or header logo.

View the [comparison sheet](04-comparison.png) or its [editable SVG](04-comparison.svg) for the icons, full lockups, palette and font stack together.

## Palette and typography

| Color | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep navy | `#07162F` | Shield, car details and Driver text |
| Racing red | `#E32636` | Crown, thought bubbles and Dreamer text |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Car silhouette |

The original live-text wordmark uses `Avenir Next, Inter, Arial, sans-serif`, in that fallback order. Font size is `66`; **Driver** has weight `700`, **Dreamer** has weight `750`, and letter spacing is `-2`. Its placement remains `x="292"`, `y="152"`, with `dx="10"` before Dreamer. The displayed typeface depends on available fonts; the SVG retains the original font stack as its source.

## Files

The original viewBoxes remain `0 0 256 256` for icons and `0 0 920 256` for full logos. Each editable SVG has a matching transparent PNG: full logos are **1840 × 512**, and icons are **768 × 768**. The comparison sheet uses white cards on a pale gray background.

Earlier rounds and application assets remain untouched. These are reviewable concept variants, without adopting a logo into the app.
