# Driver Dreamer — Straight Wide Bubbles and Thin Wide Weave, v6

This round develops v5 **Wide Soft Weave**, retaining the original v1 Grand Tourer Crest car, shield, crown and Driver Dreamer wordmark. Two variants adjust only the red rounded rectangles beneath the car.

## Variants

| Variant | Change from v5 Wide Soft Weave | Editable artwork |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **01 — Straight Wide Bubbles** | Aligns all three rectangles on the vertical centerline while retaining their width, height and corner radius. | [Full logo](01-grand-tourer-straight-wide-bubbles.svg) · [Icon](01-grand-tourer-straight-wide-bubbles-icon.svg) |
| **02 — Thin Wide Weave** | Retains the widths and alternating horizontal centers, with thinner rectangles and softer proportionate corners. | [Full logo](02-grand-tourer-thin-wide-weave.svg) · [Icon](02-grand-tourer-thin-wide-weave-icon.svg) |

Straight Wide Bubbles uses sizes `32 × 18`, `22 × 12` and `12 × 7`, corner radii `6`, `4` and `2.5`, and centers `(128, 176)`, `(128, 195)` and `(128, 210)`. Thin Wide Weave uses sizes `32 × 10`, `22 × 7` and `12 × 4`, corner radii `5`, `3.5` and `2`, and the original weave centers `(128, 176)`, `(123, 195)` and `(130, 210)`.

View the [comparison sheet](03-comparison.png) or [editable comparison SVG](03-comparison.svg) for both icons, wordmarks, enlarged thought-trail details, colors and fonts.

## Palette and typography

| Color | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep navy | `#07162F` | Shield, car details and Driver text |
| Racing red | `#E32636` | Crown, thought trail and Dreamer text |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Car silhouette |

The original live-text wordmark uses `Avenir Next, Inter, Arial, sans-serif` in that fallback order, with font size `66`, Driver weight `700`, Dreamer weight `750` and letter spacing `-2`. The displayed typeface depends on available fonts; the original SVG font stack remains the source.

## Files

Full-logo SVGs retain the `0 0 920 256` viewBox; icons retain `0 0 256 256`. Each has a matching transparent PNG: **1840 × 512** for full logos and **768 × 768** for icons. Earlier concept rounds and application assets remain untouched.
