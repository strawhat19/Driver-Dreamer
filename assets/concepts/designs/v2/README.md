# Driver Dreamer — Original Palette Typography, v2

This round pairs the two selected favorites with six new typography studies. **01 — Original Official** and **02 — Geometric GT** are exact copies of their v1 SVG and PNG assets. The official logo remains **Original Official**.

Every variant keeps the original navy, red and white palette, crest, car and thin straight bubbles. Only the six new wordmarks explore font, weight and spacing.

## Shared palette and artwork

| Color | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep navy | `#07162F` | Shield, car details and Driver text |
| Racing red | `#E32636` | Crown, thought bubbles and Dreamer text |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Car silhouette |

All wordmarks keep font size `66`, position `x="292"`, `y="152"` and `dx="10"` before Dreamer. Crest, car and bubble geometry remains fixed, so standalone icons share the same visual design.

The two favorite PNG sets are copied unchanged. New PNG previews and the comparison use the actual selected font shapes, with outlined wordmarks prepared from installed font faces. Individual logo SVGs retain editable live text and the stated fallback stacks; their displayed typeface depends on local font availability. Rounded Touring uses the system SF Rounded family `.SF NS Rounded`. No font binaries are distributed.

## Files

Each variant includes full-logo and icon SVGs with matching transparent PNGs: **1840 × 512** for full logos and **768 × 768** for icons. SVG viewBoxes remain `0 0 920 256` for full logos and `0 0 256 256` for icons. Earlier design and logo rounds remain preserved.
